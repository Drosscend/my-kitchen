# Sign in with Google through Ally

Users may log in with Google beside the e-mail and password form. The OAuth dance goes through `@adonisjs/ally`, the official AdonisJS package, which handles the state cookie and the token exchange; the application only decides which account a Google profile opens, in the `SignInWithGoogle` Action. The account keeps the Google subject in `users.google_id` rather than in a table of identities: Google is the only provider and a user links at most one Google account. Accounts are matched by subject first, never by address alone, since a Google address can change hands. A first sign-in links the account holding the same address automatically, but only when Google verified the address and the account confirmed it: linking an unconfirmed account would let whoever opened it with someone else's address keep a password on the victim's account. With an unknown address, the Action creates a confirmed account without a password, so `users.password` became nullable: such an account cannot log in with the form, sets its first password without a current one, changes its address without one, and confirms its deletion by typing its address. Google sign-in stays off while `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` or `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` is empty, so a local setup needs no Google Cloud project.
