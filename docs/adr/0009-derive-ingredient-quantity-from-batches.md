# Derive ingredient quantity from batches

One ingredient can hold stock bought on different days, each part with its own expiry date. The quantity moves from `ingredients` into `ingredient_batches`, one row per batch with an optional date and its kind, and the quantity of an ingredient is always the sum of its batches. Storing a total next to the batches would leave an invariant Postgres cannot check.

The batches belong to the `Ingredient` aggregate: the repository loads them with it and rewrites them as a whole. Unlike recipe lines, a batch keeps its raw cuid2, generated by the aggregate, across rewrites, so the interface and assistants can address it. Quantity changes keep a single rule, whatever their source: a decrease consumes the batches that expire first, an increase joins the latest batch, a dated restock adds a batch or joins the one with the same date.

The frozen state stays on the ingredient: a frozen product is another line of the pantry, and freezing suspends expiry for all its batches.
